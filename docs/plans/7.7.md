# 7.7 — metadata without RomM: task list

A library with no RomM server should look as well fed as one with it: covers, title screens,
screenshots and genres, each with the provider named, each asked once and kept. Paths are
relative to `app/src/main/java/com/tufein/valkyr/`.

Where things stand, read from the code:

- One chain decides the cover order (`util/CoverSources.kt`: ROMM, LIBRETRO, SCREENSCRAPER,
  THEGAMESDB, STEAMGRIDDB, IGDB, METADATA) with `plan()` / `settle()` / `missRecord()`, and
    `util/CoverSourcePolicy.kt` keeps a hit for 30 days and a definite miss for 3.
- A second chain already exists for non-box art: `util/CoverArtwork.kt` (LIBRETRO,
  SCREENSCRAPER, ROMM) feeding the launcher layouts in `util/FrontendCovers.kt` — title screens
  and screenshots from libretro's `Named_Titles`/`Named_Snaps` and ScreenScraper's `sstitle`/`ss`.
  It is used for launchers only, never inside the app.
- Screenshots and manuals in the app come from RomM alone: `RommGameInfo.screenshots` and
  `ManualService`, whose comment says plainly *"A manual comes only from the user's RomM server."*
- Genres already work without RomM through `GameMetadataService` (RAWG, TheGamesDB).
- Honesty about limits exists and must be reused, not reinvented: `CoverApis.Status`, the
  per-source pauses in `CoverApiGate`, ScreenScraper's pace and midnight reset in
  `ScreenScraperService`/`util/ScreenScraperQuota.kt`, the monthly TGDB allowance, and the daily
  tallies in `ui/screens/settings/covers/CoverSourcesScreen.kt`.
- `covers` (keyed console | file stem) and `game_metadata` (platform | title) survive a rescan;
  nothing deletes them. That is the behaviour new art must copy.

## 1. Screenshots and title screens inside the app, from the chain

**Why.** The artwork chain already resolves this art; the game page just never asks it.

**What.** A `game_art` table (console | stem, urls, source, fetchedAt) with the same TTL and
"never store unavailable as a miss" discipline as `covers`, filled by `CoverArtwork`'s chain, and
read by the game page and the second screen when RomM is off, missing or silent. The strip says
where its pictures came from.

**Files.** new `data/service/GameArtRepository.kt`, new entity/DAO in `data/local/`, migration in
`data/local/DogmatixDatabase.kt`, `util/CoverArtwork.kt`, `util/LibretroThumbnails.kt`,
`util/ScreenScraper.kt` (`pickArt`), `ui/screens/cloud/sections/RommGameSection.kt` (the strip
moves behind the repository), `ui/screens/game/GamePage.kt`,
`data/service/ThumbnailService.kt`/`ScreenScraperService.kt` (`findArt` reuse).

**Done when.** `GameArtTest` covers the plan, the verdicts, the TTLs and the miss records; a
device with no RomM shows screenshots for a libretro-core game and for a ScreenScraper-known
game, and a second visit makes no requests.

## 2. Say the source everywhere art is shown

**Why.** `covers.source` and the manual's origin are stored or known but not shown; only the
details line says "Data from …".

**What.** One short label under the cover and the shot strip, one line per manual, in the same
words as the settings screen uses. Long-press or the details sheet gives the full chain that was
tried (`missRecord`/`triedBy` already produce it).

**Files.** `util/CoverSources.kt` (`triedBy`), `ui/components/CoverImage.kt`,
`ui/screens/manuals/ManualsSection.kt`, `ui/screens/game/GamePage.kt`, new
`app/src/main/res/values/strings_v77_metadata.xml` plus the six locale copies.

**Done when.** `StringResourceParityTest` passes with the new keys in all seven languages, and no
art in the app is source-less.

## 3. Manuals: widen honestly, or say why not

**Why.** Manuals are the biggest gap without RomM, and also the place where provider terms
matter most.

**What.** ScreenScraper's API is the only non-RomM source in this code that can legitimately hand
out documents; add its manual media type behind a per-source switch, keep `Manuals` caps
(size per file, total cap, `.part` writes, never replacing a file), and where nothing is
available say "no manual for this game, RomM and ScreenScraper were asked" instead of silence.

**Files.** `util/ScreenScraper.kt` (media types), `data/service/ManualService.kt`,
`util/Manuals.kt`, `ui/screens/settings/ManualSettingsViewModel.kt`,
`app/src/main/res/values/strings_v77_metadata.xml`.

**Done when.** With ScreenScraper off or without a login the app says so, a fetched manual is
attributed and counted against the cap, and `ManualsTest` covers the new path.

## 4. Ask once, keep it, and do not burn the budget

**Why.** Art fields multiply requests, and quotas are shared per account or key, not per device.

**What.** Batch the new lookups through the existing gates (one `CoverApiGate` per key
fingerprint, `ScreenScraperQuota` gaps, the `Tally` counters), never re-ask a definite miss before
its TTL, and count the new requests in the same daily tallies the settings screen already shows.

**Files.** `data/service/CoverApiGate.kt`, `data/service/CoverSourceStats.kt`,
`util/ScreenScraperQuota.kt`, `data/service/GameArtRepository.kt`.

**Done when.** A 200-game shelf opened twice produces one round of requests, and the settings
screen shows the hits, misses and errors the new lookups caused.

## Order of work

1 and 2 are the visible win and need no new providers. 3 is the one with terms to respect, so it
follows and ships behind a switch. 4 is the condition everything else travels in.

## Strings, tests and the release gate

- One new `strings_v77_metadata.xml` in `values` and the same file in `values-nl`, `values-de`,
  `values-fr`, `values-es`, `values-it`, `values-pt`; `StringResourceParityTest` fails otherwise.
- Pure rules live in `util/` and get `*Test.kt` next to the existing cover tests
  (`CoverSourcesTest`, `CoverApisTest`, `CoverArtworkTest`, `ManualsTest`).
- `docs/release-notes/v7.7.0.md`, the five READMEs and `app/build.gradle.kts` move together, or
  `ReleaseDocsTest` stops the build.

## Where this runs

Tickets 1, 2 and 4 are unit-testable here. Ticket 3 needs a real ScreenScraper login, and every
ticket ends with a device check: the same library with and without RomM, compared.
