# Valkyr 9.6.0

**Find, download and organise retro games on your Android phone or handheld.** This release is the *your screenshots* theme: the pictures your emulator already keeps beside every save state become a gallery on the game page.

## What's new

- **Screenshots on the game page**: every state picture of a game in the save state folder you picked in Save sync, newest first, as a row of thumbnails (up to 24). It appears when a game has two or more; the newest still shows large under *Resume here*.
- **Full screen, made for buttons**: tap a picture or press A on it to open it. ◀ ▶ (or the Previous and Next buttons) move between pictures, B closes, and each picture is named by its slot, *Automatic save* or *Save state*, with its age.
- **Share one picture**: *Share* hands the single picture on screen to the share sheet; the receiving app gets read access to that one file only.

## Safe and honest

- Nothing is copied, moved or deleted: the gallery only reads the picture files already in the folder you picked, and without a folder or without pictures it stays out of the way.
- Not built: deleting pictures or a size cap that trims them (those files sit beside save states that emulators manage), and screenshots from emulator screenshot folders.
- No new permissions and no database change.

All new text is in the seven app languages, and the five READMEs point at this release.
